## Method

1. Generate the demo world with `seed::generate` ending at 2026-11-20 17:00 UTC (fixed, so this file is reproducible).
2. Cutoff = one week earlier. Intents and check-ins after the cutoff are hidden; events stay, since they are announced in advance.
3. For each student, every method ranks the events starting in the hidden week, as of the cutoff.
4. Events the student had already RSVP'd to (or hosts) before the cutoff are removed from both the ranking and the answer key, so methods are scored on **discovery**.
5. The answer key is the hidden-week events the student actually **checked in** to (not just RSVP'd).
6. Metrics are averaged over students with at least one such check-in, then over seeds.

Baselines: *chronological* is the pre-recommender placeholder; *popularity* ranks by campus-wide RSVPs so far. *Content-only*, *collaborative-only* and *social-only* are the hybrid's components on their own. The *validation-tuned* row uses weights from a grid search on seeds 100–105, which are disjoint from the reported seeds; it is a check that the default weights are not cherry-picked for this table. On this synthetic data the collaborative part adds little once content and friends are known; it should matter more as real history accumulates.
